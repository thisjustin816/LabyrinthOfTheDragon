## Experience

A monster pays its full XP when it's your level, up to double when it's far above you, and nothing when it's 8 or more levels below you. Goblins count one level higher than they are, zombies two, gelatinous cubes three, death knights five, the beholders ten, and the dragon twenty. Owlbears and displacer beasts count two levels lower than they are.

| Level | 4 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 75 | 99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total XP | 20 | 253 | 1,437 | 3,839 | 7,645 | 12,996 | 20,014 | 33,890 | 65,118 |

**Where XP runs out.** Floor 7's wandering monsters pay less and less after level 56 and nothing from 60. Its death knight, which counts five levels above its own for XP, holds out longest. Its elite stops paying at 58. After that, only the two beholders pay, floor 7's boss and floor 8's elite, and each fights once per run. The only way to keep leveling is to die on purpose and go down through the whole labyrinth again. From level 57, at most four more runs reach level 61, ten reach 65, and thirty reach 71. The beholders stop paying at 73. The dragon pays 680 XP, which the cleared file keeps, but it only fights once. No hero gets past level 73. The last ability upgrades below come at level 57, about as high as floor 7's wandering monsters can take you.
