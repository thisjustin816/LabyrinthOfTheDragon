# Battle

[Walkthrough](README.md) | [Heroes](heroes.md) | [Bestiary](bestiary.md)

The numbers behind battles. The [player's manual](../README.md) has the basics.

## Hitting and damage

- **Hitting.** Your ATK against the monster's DEF, or your MATK against its MDEF, sets your chance to hit: about 90% when they're equal, about 60% at worst, and about 98% at best. Monsters are far less accurate: 75% when even, from about 20% to about 95%.
- **Damage.** Damage depends on your level and the attack, and varies by up to a quarter either way. Raising your ATK makes you hit more often, but no harder.
- **Attacks that don't miss.** Magic Missile and Action Surge never miss. Fireball can't miss outright, but a bad roll halves its damage.
- **Critical hits** are the best damage rolls, about 1 in 8. A critical hit ignores a monster's resistance, and a weakness still doubles it. Attacks that hit every foe never land a critical.
- **Damage types.** Every attack has a type: physical, magical, earth, water, air, fire, light, or dark. A monster can be immune to a type (no damage), resistant (half damage), or weak (double damage). Weaknesses only count for abilities: a free basic attack does normal damage to a weak monster.

## Running away

Fleeing is a roll against the fastest monster that can still chase you. A blinded or frightened monster can't, and if none can, you always get away. Otherwise you get away half the time, plus 1 in 16 for each point of AGL you have over that monster, or minus 1 in 16 for each point it has over you, but never less than 1 in 8 or more than 7 in 8. AGL stays small all game (the monk and sorcerer reach 11 by level 47), so a gap of three or four points already matters. You can try to flee any fight, bosses included.

A monster trying to run from you makes the same roll against your AGL, and so does a frightened hero forced to flee. Bosses, elites, and the gauntlet's fights never get away.

## Random encounters

After each fight, and each time you arrive on a floor, you get a few safe steps (7 on floor 1, 6 on floor 2, and 4 from floor 3 on). After that, every step makes the next fight a little more likely until one starts. Floor 8 has no random encounters.

## Healing

- Every level up restores all your HP and SP.
- A healing ability restores its listed amount give or take a quarter, the same roll as damage, and can be a critical or a fumble. The druid's Heal always fills the bar.
- A potion restores about 3/8 of your HP, and an ether about 3/8 of your SP. An elixir restores both in full.
- A regen potion heals a quarter of your HP at the start of each of your turns for the rest of the fight. A haste potion gives more AGL than the sorcerer's own Haste.
- Floor 8 has six healing mirrors, each good for one full heal.

## Status effects in numbers

- Poison takes from an eighth to nearly a third of the victim's maximum HP each turn, depending on its strength.
- Paralysis costs the turn at least 3 times in 4.
- A confused monster always loses its turn to the confusion. A confused hero sometimes acts normally.
- Blindness drops ATK to zero.
- A prone monster's DEF drops while it's down, and nothing cures being knocked down.
- ATK up and DEF up raise the stat by a quarter.
