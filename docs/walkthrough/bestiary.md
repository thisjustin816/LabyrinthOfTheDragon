## Notes

- Quivering Palm and Disintegrate can kill any monster outright except the dragon, which takes their ordinary damage instead.
- The death knight can rise from any killing blow: an outright kill, an area attack, or a single hit.
- Frightened monsters may run from the fight, but bosses, elites, and the gauntlet's fights never get away. The gelatinous cube is the only monster fear can't touch.
- The dragon's roar can frighten you into fleeing the final battle. A remedy cures the fear, and the monk's Still Mind prevents it.
- Nothing in the game deals light or earth damage, so the weaknesses to light and the kobold's resistance to earth never come up.
