# Labyrinth of the Dragon: Player's Manual

The tunnel has collapsed behind you. There's no way back, only down, through a labyrinth of monsters, locked doors, and walls that aren't walls, to the dragon that waits at the bottom.

This game is for Game Boy Color only.

## Starting a game

There are three save files. Choose an empty file to start a new adventure, or a saved one to pick up where you left off. Each saved file shows its hero, level, floor, and time played, in hours and minutes.

A new adventure begins with choosing one of four heroes. Your hero's name is already filled in; keep it, or type a new one of up to six letters. Choose with care, because your hero is yours for the whole descent.

To erase a file, choose ERASE, then the file, then YES. An erased file can't be brought back.

## Controls

| Button | On the map | In battle |
|---|---|---|
| Control Pad | Walk | Move the cursor |
| A | Use what's in front of you | Confirm |
| B | None | Cancel |
| START | Open the menu | None |
| SELECT | None | None |

To read a sign, open a chest, pull a lever, or challenge a monster, face it and press A.

## The heroes

Four heroes are ready to take on the labyrinth. Each is rated from one to three stars in every stat. The more stars, the higher the stat and the faster it grows.

| Hero | | HP | SP | ATK | DEF | MATK | MDEF | AGL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Druid | "Heals and wards. Hard to kill." | ★★ | ★★ | ★ | ★★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★ |
| Fighter | "Hits hard and takes a beating." | ★★★ | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★ | ★★ | ★★ |
| Monk | "Strikes first, dodges often." | ★★ | ★★ | ★★ | ★★ | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ |
| Sorcerer | "Huge damage, almost no armor." | ★ | ★★★ | ★ | ★ | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★★ |

- **HP** is your health. Lose it all and you fall.
- **SP** powers your abilities. The druid and the sorcerer call it MP.
- **ATK** and **MATK** decide how often your physical and magical attacks hit.
- **DEF** and **MDEF** decide how often attacks miss you.
- **AGL** is speed. Quick heroes act sooner and get away more easily.

### Abilities

Every hero starts with a basic attack, which costs nothing, and one ability. Five more abilities are learned on the way down, and many of them grow stronger as your hero does. Most abilities that help you last until the battle ends, and most that hinder a monster wear off after a few turns. The exceptions are the monk's Evasion, whose burst of speed is brief, and the sorcerer's Sleetstorm, which keeps the monsters slipping for the whole fight.

## Exploring the labyrinth

Every floor has treasure to find, secrets to uncover, and a boss standing between you and the stairs down.

- **Signs** often hold hints, so read them all.
- **Chests** hold items and magic keys. Some are locked.
- **Doors** might need a magic key, a lever, a lit sconce, or a solved puzzle.
- **Levers** change something on the floor.
- **Monsters** guard some rooms. A boss may send you away until you're strong enough to face it.
- **Stairs down** to the next floor only go one way. Explore before you go down, because you can't come back.

### Magic keys

A magic key opens one lock and is used up. Keys you don't use come with you to the next floor.

### The menu

Press START on the map to open the menu. It shows your hero's level, experience, HP, SP, and stats, plus the floor you're on, counted down from B1 on the first floor to B8 on the last.

- **RETURN** closes the menu.
- **ITEMS** lets you use a Potion, an Ether, or an Elixir.
- **SAVE** saves your progress.
- **QUIT** goes back to the title screen without saving.

The top of the screen shows your torch's flame and the magic keys you're carrying.

### Wandering monsters

Monsters roam the halls and may attack as you walk. They keep their distance from a burning torch.

## The torch and sconces

Somewhere on the first floor lies a torch.

- **Take a flame** from a burning sconce by facing it with your torch and pressing A. The torch burns in the sconce's color.
- **Light a sconce** by facing an unlit one while your torch is burning. Some doors won't open until their sconces are lit.
- **Watch the gauge.** Every step you take burns the flame down, so take a new one before it goes out.
- **Colors** sometimes matter. Flames burn orange, green, or purple.
- **Monsters fear fire.** While your torch burns, wandering monsters leave you alone.

## Battle

Up to three monsters can attack at once. Each round, choose one of four commands:

- **FIGHT** uses your basic attack.
- **MAGIC** or **TECH** uses one of your abilities, if you have the SP. The druid and the sorcerer have MAGIC, and the fighter and the monk have TECH.
- **ITEM** opens your bag.
- **FLEE** tries to run away.

Then everyone takes a turn. The quickest usually go first.

### Fighting well

- The higher your ATK or MATK compared with a monster's defenses, the more often you'll hit.
- Some monsters are weak to certain kinds of attack and take extra damage. Others resist an attack or ignore it completely. The battle messages will tell you which.
- Now and then a blow lands as a CRITICAL HIT!
- The same monster can come in several colors, and each color is a different strength. The stronger ones are worth more experience.

### Running away

The faster you are, the easier it is to escape, and a blinded or frightened monster can't give chase. You can try to run from any battle, but a failed attempt costs your turn.

### Victory

Defeat every monster to win the battle and earn experience. A monster far weaker than you is worth very little, and one that runs away is worth nothing.

## Status effects

Heroes and monsters alike can fall under these effects. Harmful ones wear off after a few turns, and every effect ends with the battle.

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Poison | Drains HP every turn |
| Paralysis | Often keeps you from acting |
| Confusion | You may strike the wrong target or lose your turn |
| Fear | You may freeze, or run from the battle against your will |
| Blindness | Physical attacks miss far more often. Magic still works. |
| ATK, DEF, or AGL down | Lowers that stat |
| Knocked down | You lose a turn or more. A knocked-down monster is also easier to hit |

A Remedy cures every harmful effect except being knocked down.

## Items

Items are found in chests, and you can carry up to 99 of each.

| Item | Effect | On the map | In battle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Potion | Restores some HP | Yes | Yes |
| Ether | Restores some SP | Yes | Yes |
| Elixir | Restores all HP and SP | Yes | Yes |
| Remedy | Cures harmful effects | No | Yes |
| ATK Up | Raises ATK for the rest of the battle | No | Yes |
| DEF Up | Raises DEF for the rest of the battle | No | Yes |
| Regen | Restores HP every turn for the rest of the battle | No | Yes |
| Haste | Gives you Haste for the rest of the battle | No | Yes |

## Leveling up

Winning battles earns experience, and enough experience raises your level. **Each new level makes your hero stronger and restores all HP and SP.** Between battles, items are your main way to heal, so keep a few Potions handy.

## Saving

Choose SAVE from the menu to save your progress. Save often, and always before you take the stairs down.

## If you fall

If your HP runs out, you fall. You'll wake at the entrance to the first floor with your level, abilities, items, and torch, but not your magic keys, and the torch will be out. The labyrinth resets around you, with its chests full, its doors shut, and every boss back in place. Only the healing mirrors on the last floor stay used.

To go back to your last save instead, choose QUIT from the menu and load your file.

## Monsters

A few of the creatures you'll meet early on. Worse things wait higher up.

- **Kobold.** Small, scaly, and fond of fire, with an axe for close work. Kobolds are easily dazed, and sometimes trip over their own swing.
- **Goblin.** A sneaky archer with acid-tipped arrows. Not the brightest, a goblin will sometimes pick its nose instead of fighting.
- **Zombie.** The restless dead. Its bite carries poison.
- **Bugbear.** A hulking brute with javelins and a club. Its roar can shake the bravest hero.

## Hints

- Read every sign. Most of them are trying to help.
- Magic keys are precious. Think before you spend one.
- A monster that isn't in your way can still be worth the fight.
- A boss that sends you away is telling you to come back stronger.
- Save before you take the stairs down.

If you're truly stuck, the [walkthrough](walkthrough/README.md) has a map of every floor and the way through every secret. It spoils everything, so save it for when you need it.
